Transitions:
- Smooth fade between scenes (0.3-0.5 seconds)
- No abrupt cuts
- Use dissolve/cross-fade

Typography:
- Font: Modern sans-serif (Inter or similar)
- Large, readable text (minimum 48pt for body, 72pt+ for titles)
- High contrast (white/light blue text on dark background)

Color Palette:
- Background: Dark blue gradient (#0f172a to #1e293b)
- Danger/Problem: Red (#ef4444)
- Safe/Solution: Green (#10b981)
- BFF/Primary: Blue (#60a5fa)
- Text: Light gray/white (#e2e8f0)

Icons/Emojis:
- Use large, clear emojis (house, door, key, guard, thief, etc.)
- Consistent style throughout
- Animated where appropriate (pulse, float, scale)

Audio:
- Spanish narration: Neutral accent, conversational tone, moderate pace
- Background music: Soft tech/corporate instrumental, -25dB volume
- No music during narration emphasis points
- Sound effects: Subtle (door open/close, checkmark, block)

Pacing:
- Allow time for viewer to read text overlays
- Pause 0.5s after key phrases
- Emphasize "DOS verificaciones", "BLOQUEADO", "6x más rápido"

Visual Effects:
- Subtle animations (pulse, scale, float)
- No distracting effects
